## Project Title

A3_Group8B_FinalGame: Kiwi's Delivery Service

---

## Group Number

Group 8B

---

## Description

> You play as a kiwi, a flightless bird on a mission to be the best mailbird.
> Traverse through levels and move around using WASD to deliver packages to other critters.
> Interact with the environment using 'E' to receive and deliver packages.
> Balance packages carefully to not accidentally "lose" them in transit.
> Race to complete your deliveries on time, scoring well and beating your best times.

---

## Setup and Interaction Instructions

> Use `WASD` to move around and `W/SpaceBar` to jump.
> Press `E` to interact with the environment.
> Interact with the postoffice to deploy a package.
> Interact with the recipient to deliver a package.
> Press `Z` to reset the level and your time.
> Click with the mouse to navigate on-screen menus.
> Press `ESC` to return to level select.

## Iteration Notes (Post-Playtest)

### Summary of findings

We found that our play testers enjoyed our game, as we often saw them laughing, smiling, and cheering once they completed a level. The game became a social experience, as play testers would challenge and tease each other while taking turns playing.

When it came to the controls, play testers were uncertain what to do at first, but figured them out and adjusted quickly. They each had different control preferences: like using WASD or arrow keys to move, or using the restart button or walking back to the post office to retry a level. We did find an issue when a player wanted to complete a level (by pressing E to deliver the package), but accidentally pressed R, restarting their progress and causing frustration.

When it came to level design, play testers expressed wanting more challenge in variety, and excitement with the potential of new levels. When icy terrain was introduced in level 3, it was clear to some players, and unclear to others, what it was meant to be/do.

### Concrete changes

    1. Signage in tutorial level showing controls/goals

    2. Warning signage for when ice is introduced (and potentially other new mechanics if added)

    3. Less punishing time penalty for dropping packages

    4. Change restart button from R to Z

    5. Reduce target times to earn stars for more challenge
